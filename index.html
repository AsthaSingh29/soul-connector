Soul Connector — Workshop Pitch & Architecture

Complete breakdown of the 5-stage Design Thinking process, technical architecture, and the exact 2-minute pitch script.

📐 1. Design Thinking Sequence

STAGE 01
EMPATHIZE

Grief leaves unanswered questions, unshared memories, and lost personal voices.

STAGE 02
DEFINE

How might we preserve meaningful connection without claiming to replace the person?

STAGE 03
IDEATE

Consent-based interactive digital legacy vault combined with spatial memory room.

STAGE 04
PROTOTYPE

Soul Connector: WebXR A-Frame chamber with keyword memory AI & holographic avatar.

STAGE 05
TEST

Loved one speaks to Tony, asks questions about Darjeeling, and experiences closure.

⏱️ 2. The 2-Minute Pitch Breakdown

0:00 – 0:20
The Problem:
“When someone we love passes away, we don't only lose their physical presence. We lose the ability to ask them questions, hear their stories and revisit the little moments that made them who they were.”
0:20 – 0:40
The Solution:
“Soul Connector is a consent-based digital legacy platform where people can intentionally preserve their memories, voice, stories and messages during their lifetime.”
0:40 – 1:20
The Live Demo:
Show Tony’s Profile → Memory Gallery → Enter VR Memory Room → Ask: “What was our favorite trip?” → Tony responds with Darjeeling memory and speech synthesis.
1:20 – 1:40
The Empathy Boundary:
“Our goal isn't to recreate someone who has passed away. It's to preserve the connection they intentionally chose to leave behind.”
1:40 – 2:00
The Future & Finale:
Trigger 'Say Goodbye' → Tony delivers farewell monologue → Display: “Digital legacy ≠ replacing a person. It preserves what they chose to leave behind.”

💡 Judge's Technical Note

“For today's prototype, the conversational layer uses a controlled memory dataset and keyword intent matching with Web Speech synthesis. The production roadmap integrates LLM retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over encrypted personal memory vectors, while preserving strict authenticity and consent boundaries.”
